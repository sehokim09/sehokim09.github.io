About

Dr. Seho Kim is an Assistant Professor at the Hawaiʻi Institute of Geophysics and Planetology and the Hawaiʻi Space Flight Laboratory, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. The group develops microwave remote sensing techniques — bistatic radar, radio occultation and signals-of-opportunity reflectometry — and the compact RF instruments and CubeSats that fly them.

Previously NASA JPL postdoctoral fellow (2023–2025) · Ph.D., Purdue University (2023)
