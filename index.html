About

The Kim Lab at UH Mānoa does radio remote sensing from space. We use spacecraft, CubeSats and radio links, along with signals already broadcast by navigation and communication satellites and by ships at sea, to measure the ionosphere, magnetic fields in space plasma, soil moisture, sea ice and snow. The lab is led by Dr. Seho Kim, Assistant Professor at the Hawaiʻi Institute of Geophysics and Planetology and Faculty Member of the Hawaiʻi Space Flight Laboratory.

Previously NASA JPL postdoctoral fellow (2023–2025) · Ph.D., Purdue University (2023)
