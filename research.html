Mission

HITechSat

The HITechSat (HTS) program develops rapidly integrable CubeSat platforms for flying new RF instruments. Its first spacecraft, HITechSat-1, is a 3U CubeSat organized into six subsystems — structure and thermal, on-board computer and flight software, electrical power, attitude determination and control, communications and ground station, and payload.

HTS-1 is also a training ground: senior capstone students design and build its subsystems.

Project

FRAMES

FRAMES monitors the ionosphere and magnetic field using radio measurements — Faraday rotation, total electron content (TEC) retrieval and dual-frequency methods.

Technique

Signals of Opportunity

Signals-of-opportunity reflectometry (SoOp-R) and GNSS reflectometry reuse transmissions already in orbit — navigation and communication satellites — as bistatic radar sources. Delay-Doppler mapping of the reflected signals lets small, low-power receivers measure soil moisture, snow, sea ice and other surface properties.

Science

Planetary radio science

Radio occultation and planetary radar probe atmospheres, ionospheres and magnetic fields beyond Earth — including using Faraday rotation in spacecraft radio links to study Jupiter's magnetic field.
